About this map. Boroughs are shaded either by their total number of publicly maintained trees, or by trees per square kilometre. Raw totals favour large boroughs, so the density view gives a fairer comparison: switching to it reshuffles the ranking. Figures come from the Greater London Authority's public realm tree inventory (… trees in total). Some boroughs, notably Croydon, Havering and the City of London, appear very low because they released only partial data, not because they lack trees. Borough areas are computed from the boundary polygons. Data: London Datastore, under the UK Open Government Licence.
